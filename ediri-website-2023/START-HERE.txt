IMAGES
photos/webp/
Fourteen web-optimised activity images in WebP format.

photos/jpeg/
The same fourteen images as widely compatible JPEG files.

The full-resolution PNG master copies are retained in the original
EDIRI_2023_Website_Upload_Package.zip. They are omitted from this lightweight
upload edition because the website uses the JPEG and WebP files.

All fourteen activity images were extracted from the supplied Word file.
They comprise thirteen photographs and one workshop flipchart image.
Edits use the original photographic pixels: restrained exposure, colour
and sharpness adjustments, with permanent expanded face blur. The final
package contains no AI-generated or reconstructed photographs.

Children's faces and possible minors' faces have been obscured, including
small and partly hidden faces. Child photographs visible in background
posters were also obscured. Some nearby adult faces are included in
conservative blur areas. The private contact list on the radio-studio
monitor has been obscured. The blur is flattened into every delivered
image and into the report's embedded photographs.

Web exports retain the original framing and do not enlarge low-resolution
source files. Their longest edge is capped at 1,600 pixels. Source location
and camera metadata are excluded.
