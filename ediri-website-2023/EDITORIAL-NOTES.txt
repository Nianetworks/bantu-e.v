EDIRI REPORT EDITORIAL HANDOVER

Source documents
- PROJECT PROGRESS REPORT 9Nov 2023 EzraAbi.docx
- Cerebral Palsy EDIRI Pilot Project Progress Report.pdf

The original Word report was used to reconcile activity figures and field
observations. The supplied PDF provided the second report version but
contained placeholders where photographs should appear.

The public report has been rewritten in clear British English, organised
by activity and illustrated with all fourteen extracted activity images.
The eight-page PDF and Word editions present the same material. The
website article reproduces that content with a responsive photo gallery.

Accuracy decisions
- The implementation period remains June to September 2023. Preparation
  in November 2026 does not imply that these are current activity results.
- The original Word narrative resolves the contradictory workshop
  attendance wording: 26 health workers attended against a target of 40.
- Stakeholder attendance is 40, primary health worker training attendance
  is 26, and hospital sensitization attendance is 35. These are reported
  separately because overlap between participants is not established.
- Home follow-up reached 38 children. This is not stated as 38 visits;
  the report says all planned visits were completed without a visit count.
- Three outreaches were completed. Radio programming is described without
  inventing a count of broadcasts or listeners.
- Equipment is described as procured. The report does not add quantities,
  named recipients or distribution totals that the Word source does not give.
- Increased demand for services is attributed to the team's observations.
  No quantified long-term health or functional outcome is invented.
- Workshop flipchart wording is preserved in the image and labelled as
  participants' discussion material rather than clinical guidance.
- The hospital name 'Devine Mercy Hospital' follows the supplied reports.

Privacy and image preparation
- The user chose direct photographic edits on the originals. All final
  assets use those originals, without generative reconstruction.
- Strong, expanded blur is permanently applied to children's heads and
  faces and to possible minors where appropriate. Some adult faces were
  also covered conservatively. Small child images in background posters
  were obscured, as was a private contact list visible on a monitor.
- Both web formats contain the same privacy edits. The full-resolution PNG
  masters remain available in the original package and are omitted here.
- No unblurred activity image is included in the report or upload package.
- Original GPS and camera metadata are not included in the photo exports.
- Current Bantu e.V. brand artwork is used; the other four logos are
  preserved from the original report without redrawing them.

Quality checks
- Every report page was rendered and visually reviewed.
- All fourteen final activity images were reviewed, with close inspection
  of the photographs containing children and babies.
- Captions and alternative text are supplied for all activity images.
- Desktop and phone-width article previews were visually reviewed. The
  phone-width layout had no horizontal page overflow and no missing alt text.
- The public article has a real HTML results table with headers and a
  caption, responsive images, JPEG fallbacks and descriptive alternatives.

This handover note is for the website editor; it is not public article copy.
