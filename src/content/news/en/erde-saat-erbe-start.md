date: 2026-09-01
type: news
summary: "Our new BIKO project brings rammed earth, seeds and colonial traces into Berlin schools, in cooperation with AfricAvenir International e.V."
project: p26-224-erde-saat-erbe
---

From September to December 2026, three learning groups from grades 9 to 11 explore colonial continuities in building and farming: they build a rammed-earth element, sow and observe, explore colonial traces in the African Quarter in Wedding and present their results in a mini exhibition at their school.

Funded by the State of Berlin, Senate Department for Education, Youth and Family, through the Stiftung Nord-Süd-Brücken (BIKO programme).
