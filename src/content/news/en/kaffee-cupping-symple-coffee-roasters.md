Under the motto *“The journey behind every bean”*, we invited people to a cupping session with Ugandan coffee on 28 September 2026: together with our partner **Symple Coffee Roasters** at their café on Sredzkistraße in Berlin, and in cooperation with **Regenerate Africa**.

At a cupping, coffee is brewed following fixed rules and tasted with a spoon, so that origin and flavour can be compared. Around the long table we tasted, compared and talked a lot about the coffee's journey: from the highlands of Uganda, the “Pearl of Africa”, to a cup in Berlin.

Thank you to the team at Symple Coffee Roasters and to everyone who came! We work with Symple on our coffee projects. You can find our own coffee brand in the [shop under ABANTU COFFEE](/en/shop/#coffee).

*Taste. Discover. Connect.*
