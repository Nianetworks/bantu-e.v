We have worked with ABI in Uganda since 2021: on the Ruhaama Community Resource Centre, the OURS project for children with cerebral palsy, the youth exchange *Okushoma kwa Boona* and now the Pearl Kindergarten in Kampala.

ABI was founded in 2017 as *Ability Beyond the Iris*: the name expressed the conviction that “seemingly weak” people have abilities far beyond what others see in them. In 2026 the organisation renamed itself **Anchored Bridges Initiatives**. The new name shows that ABI now works holistically in community development: education and youth, livelihoods, health and peacebuilding. Inclusion of people with disabilities remains a cross-cutting theme in all programmes.
