On 29 October 2026, Bantu e.V. and our Ugandan partner Anchored Bridges Initiatives (ABI) signed the project agreement for the **Pearl Kindergarten Kampala**.

The Montessori-inspired kindergarten in Namuwongo offers 60 children aged three to six a place with a warm meal every day; families contribute a small, socially graded cost share towards the project costs. Six local educators are being trained. The project runs until the end of March 2027 and is funded by the EZ-Kleinprojektefonds of the Stiftung Nord-Süd-Brücken.

Bantu e.V. contributes €1,390 of its own funds. Every donation with the reference “Pearl Kita” helps.
