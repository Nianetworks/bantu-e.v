Why do some children have access to good education and others do not, in Kampala as in Berlin? Our new training series is for teachers, educators, school social workers, diaspora activists, parents and people active in associations.

- **Sat 28 Nov 2026:** Educational justice in global comparison
- **Sat 5 Dec 2026:** The right to education and inclusive education
- **Sat 12 Dec 2026:** Education financing and room for local action
- **Sat 19 Dec 2026:** Bringing educational justice into your own practice, with closing session

Registration closes on the Tuesday before each date. Full details on the project page.

Funded by ENGAGEMENT GLOBAL with funds from the BMZ (“Gefördert durch ENGAGEMENT GLOBAL mit Mitteln des BMZ”).
