After seven years of work as an association, it was time for a fresh start online. On the new website you will find:

- our **programmes**: international cooperation and work in Germany, with all ongoing and completed projects,
- **news and events**,
- **success stories**, our **annual and transparency reports** since 2020 and a **downloads** section,
- the **people** behind Bantu e.V. and our partner organisations,
- soon a small **shop** whose proceeds support our projects.

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no fonts from third-party servers. We welcome your feedback: [info@bantu-ev.org](mailto:info@bantu-ev.org).
