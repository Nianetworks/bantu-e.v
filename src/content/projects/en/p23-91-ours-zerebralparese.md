In rural Uganda, children with cerebral palsy are often diagnosed late or not at all – precious time for support is lost, and families are left alone with stigma.

In the **OURS** project, our partner ABI organised early detection and early intervention in Mbarara and other districts of south-western Uganda:

- **172 children** – more than the planned 150 – received medical rehabilitation.
- Parents set up **self-help groups** to support each other.
- The work reached **six districts**.

Inclusion of people with disabilities remains a cross-cutting theme in all of ABI’s programmes and in our joint work.
