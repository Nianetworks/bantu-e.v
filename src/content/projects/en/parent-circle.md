**Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network**

Parents need spaces where their experiences are taken seriously and different family realities are welcome. ParentCircle focused on Black, Indigenous and parents of colour in Berlin.

## Sharing everyday life

The initiative combined regular discussions with shared activities such as cooking and opportunities for children to play. Parents could exchange experiences of parenting, cultural identity and everyday racism. Individual support was envisaged when needed.

## Responsibility and collaboration

The historical project phase documented here was implemented by tubman.network under Bantu e.V.’s legal and administrative sponsorship. The thematic images illustrate the idea of a parents’ circle. This archive page does not advertise current gatherings.
