**Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network**

YouthECO brought young people, women and partner organisations together across Europe around sustainability and eco-entrepreneurship. Training, mentoring and learning resources aimed to strengthen skills and improve access to sustainable activities and business ideas.

## European cooperation

Aspire Education Group GmbH coordinated the European consortium. Bantu e.V. provided legal and administrative sponsorship for the German contribution implemented by the tubman.network team. The Erasmus+ project also involved partners from Greece, Malta, Cyprus, Slovenia and Poland.

The consortium:

- SYMPLEXIS (Greece)
- Friends of the Earth Malta (Malta)
- Research and Education of Social Empowerment and Transformation (RESET) Ltd (Cyprus)
- Izobraževalni center Geoss d.o.o. (Slovenia)
- Polish Farm Advisory and Training Centre (Poland)
- tubman.network under the legal and administrative sponsorship of Bantu e.V. (Germany): forest gardens and storytelling on Black engagement

## Conference in Berlin

The YouthECO conference took place at Berlin Global Village on 18 October 2024. Presentations and encounters brought the project’s themes into shared discussion. The gallery contains published photographs of that event.

More about the project: [youtheco.eu](https://youtheco.eu)

This page records the historical German project contribution; it does not present Bantu e.V. as coordinator of the whole European consortium.
