The European **Youth Eco Project** prepared young people for the green economy, especially young women and young people not in education, employment or training (NEETs). Training, mentoring and materials covered eco-entrepreneurship, sustainability practice and leadership. The project was coordinated by Aspire Education Group GmbH. Bantu e.V. was the partner for Germany; the Tubman.Network team carried out the work.

## Consortium

- SYMPLEXIS (Greece)
- Friends of the Earth Malta (Malta)
- Research and Education of Social Empowerment and Transformation (RESET) Ltd (Cyprus)
- Izobraževalni center Geoss d.o.o. (Slovenia)
- Polish Farm Advisory and Training Centre (Poland)
- Bantu e.V. with the Tubman.Network team (Germany): forest gardens and storytelling on Black engagement

## Youth Eco Conference, 18 October 2024

The Tubman.Network team co-organised and co-hosted the conference at **Berlin Global Village**. It brought together the European partners, young people and women educators from Black Engagement Matters to share knowledge on eco-literacy, sustainability and leadership. More about the project: [youtheco.eu](https://youtheco.eu)
