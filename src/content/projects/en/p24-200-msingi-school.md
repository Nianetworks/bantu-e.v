Young women in Mbarara often have little access to recognised vocational training, and therefore to an income of their own. Together with the **Nyentsya Community Development Association (NCDA)** we made hairdressing and cosmetology training possible at Msingi School of BTVET.

## Results

- **18 young women** (aged 18–35) completed the full 24-week training.
- **All 18** passed their theoretical and practical final exams and received certificates.
- **7 graduates** were hired directly by their placement businesses: an immediate employment rate of **39%**.
- All participants took part in a health workshop (HIV/AIDS prevention) and two workshops on business management and financial planning.
- The salon equipment stays on site as the basis for future cohorts and salon services.
- Participants spontaneously formed informal mutual-support groups.

> “Your certificates are not merely a piece of paper. They are proof that you have the skills, the courage and the determination to make your way. I am proud of you.”
>
> Boaz Murinzi Murema, Executive Director, Transparency Report 2025

The project budget of €11,220.74 was kept, with €11,190.70 spent. We thank the Stiftung Nord-Süd-Brücken, the Schmitz-Stiftungen and above all the entire teaching team of Msingi School for their outstanding work on the ground.
