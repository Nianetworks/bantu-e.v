The UN International Decade for People of African Descent (2015–2024) put recognition, justice and development for Black people worldwide at its centre. As the decade closed, Bantu e.V. used **Black Engagement Matters** to strengthen the volunteer engagement of Black communities in Germany.

- **10 Black resource persons** were trained to carry knowledge and structures into their communities.
- **30 Black organisations** from across Germany were connected with each other.
- A **closing conference** brought the results together and strengthened cooperation beyond the project.
