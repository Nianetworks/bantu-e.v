**Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network**

Black associations and community initiatives make an important contribution to society. Sustained civic engagement also needs reliable structures, access to knowledge and space to discuss racism and discrimination.

## Strengthening organisations

Black Engagement Matters: Building Black Resilience addressed those foundations. Its themes included organisational leadership, legal questions, fundraising, strategic planning, public relations, volunteer management and data protection.

Empowerment, discrimination-sensitive support, and safer and braver spaces complemented the organisational topics. The approach connected professional learning with knowledge held within communities.

## Networks and lasting support

The project plan envisaged training Black resource persons and exchange across organisations towards the close of the UN International Decade for People of African Descent, 2015–2024. Its aim was to make knowledge and mutual support available within Black civil society over the longer term.

## Responsibility and collaboration

Bantu e.V. provided the legal and administrative project sponsorship; tubman.network was responsible for implementation. The German Foundation for Engagement and Volunteering supported the project. Supplementary thematic images illustrate collaboration, learning and networking.
