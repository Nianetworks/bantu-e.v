The UN International Decade for People of African Descent (2015–2024) put recognition, justice and development for Black people worldwide at its centre. As the decade closed, Bantu e.V. used **Black Engagement Matters** to strengthen the volunteer engagement of Black communities in Germany and, with it, social cohesion.

- **Training based on a needs assessment**: organisational leadership, legal matters, fundraising, strategic planning, public relations, volunteer management, data protection, safer and braver spaces, empowerment, structural racism and discrimination-sensitive support.
- **10 Black resource persons** were trained so that Black associations can rely on culturally sensitive advice in the long term.
- **All activities were led by BIPoC experts.**
- A **closing conference** at the end of the UN Decade connected activists from **30 Black organisations** across Germany and looked at what makes Black civic engagement succeed.

## Implementation

A project of Bantu e.V., implemented by the Tubman.Network team. Tubman.Network worked under the umbrella of Bantu e.V. until January 2024 and is now the independent association [tubman.network e.V.](https://tubman.network).
