Why do some children have access to good education and others do not, in Kampala as in Berlin? This training series shows how barriers to education in Uganda and Germany are connected: colonial education structures, unequal resources, exclusion based on disability, gender and origin. Experts from Anchored Bridges Initiatives join live from Kampala on every day. Together we develop realistic next steps for your own educational or community practice.

## Dates (each day can be booked separately)

| Date | Topic |
|---|---|
| Sat 28 Nov 2026 | Educational justice in global comparison |
| Sat 5 Dec 2026 | The right to education and inclusive education |
| Sat 12 Dec 2026 | Education financing and room for local action |
| Sat 19 Dec 2026 | Bringing educational justice into your own practice, with closing session |

Each day **10:00–15:00**, on site in an accessible venue in Berlin (address sent with your confirmation) or online via Zoom. Free of charge, with lunch on site, a certificate of attendance and free childcare (please mention it when registering).

## Who is it for?

- Teachers, early-years educators, school social workers and other education professionals in Berlin and Brandenburg
- People active in the African and global diaspora, especially aged 18 to 35
- Parents with links to countries of the Global South
- Members of associations, NGOs and self-organised groups
