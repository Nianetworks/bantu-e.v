From 2021 until January 2024, Bantu e.V. was the **fiscal host of Tubman.Network** – a network supporting Black parents, families and communities. In January 2024 Tubman.Network was registered as an independent non-profit association, and we handed over the remaining project funds.

## Safer Spaces for Mothers – Mama Circle (2023–2024)

Funded by the Robert Bosch Stiftung, African and Afro-diasporic mothers who had fled war met every Sunday in the **Mama Circle** in Berlin:

- childcare during the meetings,
- psychosocial counselling four days a week,
- accompaniment to medical and official appointments and everyday support.

The result: a stable social network and stronger mother–child bonds.

## ParentCircle

At weekly meetings, BIPoC parents found community, mutual support and shared strategies for dealing with everyday discrimination.
