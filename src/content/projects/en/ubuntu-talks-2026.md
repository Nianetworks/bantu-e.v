Berlin-Mitte is a diverse district: people from East Africa and across the African continent live door to door with long-standing neighbours, yet everyday exchange often doesn’t happen. And within the diaspora, the knowledge of the older generation and the lives of young people who grew up in Germany rarely meet in a safe setting.

**Ubuntu Talks** starts here. The name draws on the Ubuntu philosophy: *“I am because we are.”* Participants shape their neighbourhood. They are not recipients of help.

## Programme on Sunday, 22 November 2026

| Time | Programme |
|---|---|
| 11:00 | Doors open · space for prayer and reflection (voluntary, open to all faiths and worldviews) |
| 12:00 | Official start with Bantu e.V. and FUG: welcome and the idea of Ubuntu |
| 12:30 | Round 1: Arriving in Berlin-Mitte, story circles |
| 13:45 | Lunch break with a finger-food buffet |
| 14:15 | Round 2: What is Ubuntu: humanity in a polarised society? |
| 15:30 | Topic tables: generations, family, work and authorities, health, faith, neighbourhood |
| 16:45 | Harvest: what do we take with us? We agree on an Ubuntu circle that carries on. |
| 17:00 | Culture dinner: bring a dish from your community (with an ingredients card for people with allergies); there is also a warm dinner for everyone |
| 18:30 | Closing circle with music, short feedback by QR code (2 minutes, no name needed) |
| 19:00 | End |

All day: coffee, tea and snacks · childcare by a qualified educator (11:00–19:00) · safe space for confidential conversations · quiet room · German/English interpretation, other languages and sign language on request.

## Taking part

Free entry. Room 2.02 at VHS Berlin Mitte is step-free. Online participation via a closed video conference (link after registration); there is no livestream. **Registration:** with the [online form](https://forms.gle/fBRsqho22GvvBTL1A) (Google Forms), by e-mail to [info@bantu-ev.org](mailto:info@bantu-ev.org?subject=Registration%20Ubuntu%20Talks) or by phone at +49 176 3421 3765.

Trust first: photos and videos are taken only with consent; anyone who does not want to be filmed or photographed gets a red band. Conversation circles and the safe space are never recorded.

Everything at a glance: [see the flyer](/en/news/ubuntu-talks-22-11-2026/#flyer) (front and back as pictures on the event page, German and English).

The day aims to start a lasting **Ubuntu circle** that keeps meeting after the project ends.
