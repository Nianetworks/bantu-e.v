Uganda livelihoods programme launched in 2017

Working through women’s groups and our Ugandan partner Anchored Bridges Initiatives (ABI), formerly Ability Beyond the Iris, the initiative supported goat rearing, pig rearing and beekeeping to strengthen family incomes. The programme directly supported 50 families. The project team reports that more than 26 of those families moved out of poverty.

The work aimed to help families meet everyday needs, ease financial pressures within households and contribute to the prevention of domestic violence. Women’s participation as producers and organisers was central to the programme.

Photographs from the project archive document livestock shelters, pig rearing and traditional beehives. The programme’s reported progress provides a basis for continued support for women’s economic opportunities and family livelihoods.
