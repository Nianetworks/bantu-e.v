ABI UGANDA REPORTS EDITORIAL HANDOVER

Sources
The user's project account and clarifications are the sources for programme
years, participant totals, reported poverty outcomes, school progression,
the two-container total, locations, and the intended use of the funds.
The 163 supplied photographs document activities, objects, people and
visible cheque wording. They do not independently establish impact totals.

School context was also checked against the school’s English department:
https://wrg-online.de/englisch
The school describes the Uganda project as part of intercultural learning
in selected English classes. Its official name is Wilhelm-Remy-Gymnasium
Bendorf. No unrelated material from search results was used.

Names
Use Anchored Bridges Initiatives (ABI), formerly Ability Beyond the Iris,
as specifically requested. The historic wording on the photographed
cheques is preserved without retouching it.
Royal College Namugongo is the secondary and parent school of Royal Junior
Namugongo. The user identified the older students in white school shirts
as Royal College students. The six pupils whose fees were supported
attended Royal Junior Namugongo. The exact fee-payment period was not
specified, so that report does not invent start/end dates.

Dates
The livelihoods programme is dated to 2017 from the user's account.
Some related images have 2018 WhatsApp filenames. A file-sharing date is
not automatically a capture date or evidence of a new project phase.
The English classroom display has an EXIF date of 24 June 2017.
The Royal College cheque visibly bears the date 18 September 2018.
The Rwengoma cheque visibly bears the date 11 October 2018, despite a
November 2018 filename. Its printed date is used in the report caption.
The craft photos have July 2017 capture dates. They are explicitly labelled
as earlier archival context for the support the user says continued from
2018; they are not relabelled as 2018 photographs.

Funding
Approximately EUR 7,000 was one pupil-led fundraising pool. The school
partnership, the Rwengoma allocation and the six pupils' fee support are
reported separately, without multiplying the donation three times.
The German cheque appears to show a figure a little above EUR 7,000, so
the reports use the rounded description supplied by the user.
The Rwengoma cheque records UGX 1,000,000. A Royal College cheque also
records UGX 1,000,000. The reports do not treat either as a full budget,
invent a currency conversion, or assign a cost to each ramp or pupil.

Reported results
The livelihoods account says 50 families received direct support and more
than 26 moved out of poverty. No poverty threshold, survey method or
time interval was supplied, so no independent evaluation is claimed.
Domestic-violence prevention is framed as an aim of the livelihoods work,
not a measured reduction or a claim that poverty is its sole cause.
The mobility account says two 40-foot containers and more than 600
beneficiaries across Ntungamo, Jinja and Kasubi. Return to school and work
is described as a reported outcome without inventing numerical rates.
The six Royal Junior pupils' primary completion and secondary progression
are reported from the user's account, without fabricating individual
graduation dates or publicising the names printed on old pupil portraits.
Rwengoma garden harvests, meal totals and dropout changes are not invented.
The craft shop is described as forthcoming, as stated by the user.

Photo selection
All 163 files were reviewed. The current package contains 31 distinct
selected images. The six individual pupil portraits in the user-confirmed
report are included as edited crops with light face blur and the printed
personal details removed. The prior pupil group photo and the removed
craft handshake photo are excluded. Near-identical alternatives and
unclear school/meeting images remain outside the selected publication set.
The school gathering used with the Rwengoma account is associated by
setting and archive grouping; its caption does not assert an exact school
or date. The archive does not include a confirmed vegetable-garden image.
The unselected files remain catalogued in all-163-photo-index.csv.

Images use direct photographic adjustments rather than generative edits.
The exposure of some 2018 distribution images was already clipped in the
source. Restrained correction improves presentation without inventing
missing detail. There is no artificial enlargement of small originals.
Children's faces and possible minors' faces were given the requested
lighter blur and reviewed in context. Light blur is not full anonymity.
GPS and camera metadata are omitted from delivered photos.
