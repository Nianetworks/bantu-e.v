# ABANTU COFFEE: Signs of Connection
## Cultural references and original artwork | 9 November 2026

These are contemporary design interpretations. Adinkra references belong to Akan traditions associated with Ghana; see the [Smithsonian context](https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/moments/kwanzaa). Kwanzaa is an African American and Pan-African celebration, created in 1966. Neither is presented here as a Ugandan tradition. The artistic reference does not establish where a coffee was grown, who produced it, or any certification.

| Ubuntu | Unity Cup | Original contemporary vessel drawing inspired by Kikombe cha Umoja, the unity cup, with Mkeka-inspired woven foundation marks. [Official Kwanzaa symbols](https://www.officialkwanzaawebsite.org/the-symbols.html). |
| Kukunda | Akoma Ntoso | Four connected heart-like arms; understanding and agreement. [University of Michigan](https://prod.lsa.umich.edu/daas/engagement/adinkra_symbols.html). |

The short explanations above are design interpretations, not exhaustive accounts of each tradition. The new shapes were drawn for this collection; they are not scans of historical stamps. Original motifs are deliberately distinguished from named cultural references. Before committing a named symbol to manufactured packaging, have its final drawing and accompanying wording reviewed with a knowledgeable cultural practitioner.

Additional context: [Smithsonian NMAAHC on Kwanzaa](https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/moments/kwanzaa).

## Commercial visual references

[Afro Coffee](https://afrocoffee.com/de/) informed the ambition for confident colour, full-bag print and large graphic forms. Its logos, exact illustrations, patterns, product language and claims have not been reused. Earlier references included [Coffee Circle](https://www.coffeecircle.com/de/c/kaffee-shop) for readable product hierarchy. Neither reference is a partner or endorser.

The user-supplied IMG_2091.jpg informed chevrons, dot groupings, contrast and woven rhythm. New compositions were created rather than tracing that entire textile.

## Owner and partner

ABANTU COFFEE is presented as a project by Bantu e.V., with Balambuli Kwigate Coffee (BKC) as the main coffee partner, following the user's instruction for all twelve editions. Original supplied marks are included. No other partner logos or certification marks have been invented.
