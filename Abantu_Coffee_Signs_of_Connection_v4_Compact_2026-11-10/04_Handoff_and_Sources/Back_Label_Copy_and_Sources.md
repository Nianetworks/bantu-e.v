# Back-label copy and sources
9 November 2026

## Roots & Routes

Source: https://www.bantu-ev.org/en/shop/abantu-coffee-roots-and-routes/

The page confirms Sankofa Heritage Coffee, Mount Elgon in Uganda, medium-roast Arabica and 250 g. The label's short German story connects the Sankofa idea with coffee-growing communities and the diaspora. The project paragraph paraphrases selected intended goals: fairer income, training and educational opportunities. It makes no claim that these outcomes have already been achieved or that a fixed share of each pack is donated.

## Sebei Elgon

Source: https://www.bantu-ev.org/en/shop/abantu-coffee-sebei-elgon/

The page confirms Mountain Heritage Coffee, the eastern slopes of Mount Elgon in Uganda, and 250 g. The German back copy draws on the page's cultural-heritage, youth-development and high-altitude-farming goals. No roast, Arabica/Robusta claim or tasting note has been added. The proposed cooperative relationship mentioned online is not presented on the label as a confirmed supply arrangement.

## Remaining ten backs

Kanungu Forest's stated coffee details come from the previously supplied brief. The nine other concept backs contain artwork narratives and a collection-development description, without new supplier or coffee specifications. Their QR targets are proposed edition-specific pages that currently return 404. Publishing those pages remains a separate website task.

The cultural details follow Cultural_References_and_Artwork_Key.md. Adinkra references are Akan; the Ubuntu cup drawing is a contemporary Kwanzaa-inspired design. None is presented as a Ugandan historical symbol. The original pastoral, step, summit and river motifs are described as newly drawn graphic work.

## Production fields

The MHD/lot and roaster/operator fields are left blank rather than populated with invented production details. They must be completed for the actual product. This is a graphic design set with source-controlled copy, not a final manufacturing approval.
