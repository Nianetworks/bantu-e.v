Our work

Work in Germany

In Berlin and across Germany we combine global learning with empowerment: education projects in schools, spaces of encounter for the African diaspora and their neighbours, advice for refugees and migrants, and fiscal sponsorship for young initiatives.

Education

Global learning, educational justice and cross-cultural exchange: in Germany and Uganda we dismantle historical biases and make learning accessible: for school classes, young people and the wider public.

Education: programme page

General education work

Workshops, trainings and political-education seminars that we offer together with AfricAvenir International e.V.: on global justice, anti-racism, colonial history and the Sustainable Development Goals. We also offer process support, facilitation and mediation for organisations. Schools, associations, public bodies and teams can book formats with us.

Programme 5 · Advice and support for refugees

Migration & Integration

We support, advise and accompany refugees and migrants building their lives in Germany: culturally sensitive, multilingual and aware of intersectional discrimination.

Migration & Integration: programme page

Procedural advice

Hands-on advice for migrants and refugees on asylum and residence procedures and administrative processes: we help people understand letters and decisions, keep track of deadlines, prepare applications and find the right authorities. For legal representation we refer to specialised lawyers and counselling services.

Approach

How we work

  • Global learning with voices from the Global South, joining live
  • Low-barrier formats: free of charge, with childcare and interpretation
  • Communities shape the work themselves. They are not recipients of help
  • Cooperation with schools, districts and partner associations
