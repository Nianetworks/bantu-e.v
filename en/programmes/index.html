Fiscal sponsorship

We enable initiatives

As a fiscal sponsor we lend young initiatives our charitable status, administration and experience, from applying for and managing grants to financial reporting, until they can work independently.

Berlin · 2022–2024

tubman.network e.V.

tubman.network works with children, young people, families and communities of African and Afro-diasporic heritage: through education, psychosocial support, empowerment and the arts. Tubman.Network began as an initiative under the umbrella of Bantu e.V. and is now an association of its own (VR 40776 B). For the historical projects listed below, Bantu e.V. provided the legal and administrative project sponsorship: we managed the grants, prepared the financial reports and handled staff and contracts; the tubman.network team implemented them. Each project had its own duration. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

Historical projects under the legal and administrative sponsorship of Bantu e.V., implemented by tubman.network

  • Ubuntu for All and Equal Rights for All (2022–2023, DSEE, Deutsche Fernsehlotterie / Deutsches Hilfswerk)
  • FashionEmpowerment (2023, Berlin-Mitte District Office)
  • Safer Spaces for Mothers and Mama Circle (1 Apr 2023 to 31 Jul 2024, Robert Bosch Stiftung)
  • Black Engagement Matters: Building Black Resilience (2023–2024, DSEE)
  • Youth Eco Project and YouthECO conference (2024, Erasmus+)
  • ParentCircle

Sandau (Elbe), Sachsen-Anhalt · since 2022

Kikili e.V. i.G.

In Sandau (Elbe), the smallest town in Saxony-Anhalt, Kikili is reviving the listed former town granary (“Stadtspeicher”) as an inclusive “third place”: with remembrance culture, education and cultural activities for young and old and a platform for initiatives working on social justice, environmental protection and inclusion. The project is managed fully and independently by Ms Vicky Germain as developer (Bauherr*in) and project lead. Bantu e.V. is its fiscal sponsor and provides charitable status and administration until Kikili is registered as an association of its own.

Projects under our sponsorship

  • Rescue and revival of the Sandau town granary
  • Heritage Open Day 2026 (13.09.2026): landscape drawing workshop and concert
