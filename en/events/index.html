School projectSeptember–December 2026

Soil, Seed and Heritage: workshops at Berlin schools

Three learning groups from grades 9 to 11 build a rammed-earth element, sow and observe, explore colonial traces in the African Quarter in Wedding and present their results in a mini exhibition at their school. In cooperation with AfricAvenir International e.V.

  • Where: Berlin schools

For the participating school classes, not open to the public.

2026

  1. 28 September

    TastingWhere: Symple Coffee Roasters, Sredzkistraße 45, 10435 Berlin

    Ugandan Coffee Cupping Session: The journey behind every bean

    A tasting of Ugandan coffees with our partner Symple Coffee Roasters, in cooperation with Regenerate Africa. Our guests included Robert, Director of Rugyeyo Farm in Uganda, and Ronald from the Ugandan Embassy.

    Read the post: Ugandan Coffee Cupping Session: The journey behind every bean

  2. 13 September

    CultureWhere: Sandau (Elbe), Saxony-Anhalt

    Heritage Open Day at the Sandau town granary

    A landscape drawing workshop and a concert in the former town granary. Part of the Kikili project, for which Bantu e.V. is the fiscal sponsor.

    About the project: Heritage Open Day at the Sandau town granary

2025

  1. June

    CeremonyWhere: Msingi School of BTVET, Mbarara, Uganda

    Certificate ceremony at Msingi School

    After 24 scheduled project weeks of hairdressing and cosmetology training, the trainees presented their work and received their certificates: all 18 passed, and seven were hired straight away by their placement salons. With our partner organisation NCDA.

    Read the post: Certificate ceremony at Msingi SchoolAbout the project: Certificate ceremony at Msingi School

  2. 15 May–6 Jun

    ExchangeWhere: Online

    Okushoma kwa Boona: digital return exchange

    Because five of the six Ugandan participants were refused Schengen visas, the return visit took place online: with virtual visits to our partner schools in Berlin, Bendorf, Andernach and Neuwied and a joint closing and reflection phase.

    About the project: Okushoma kwa Boona: digital return exchange

  3. 24 Jan–30 Mar

    WorkshopWhere: Online

    Okushoma kwa Boona: five online weekend workshops

    The participants from Germany and Uganda deepened their analysis of Sustainable Development Goal 4 (quality and inclusive education) and prepared the digital return exchange.

    About the project: Okushoma kwa Boona: five online weekend workshops

2024

  1. November

    ExchangeWhere: Germany

    A visit from Uganda to our partner schools

    Only one of the Ugandan participants of Okushoma kwa Boona, a participant from our partner organisation ABI, was granted a visa. She visited German partner schools and educational institutions and shared experience of inclusive education.

    About the project: A visit from Uganda to our partner schools

  2. 18 October

    ConferenceWhere: Berlin Global Village, Berlin

    Youth Eco Conference

    The conference of the European Youth Eco Project brought together the partners from six countries, young people and women educators from Black Engagement Matters to share knowledge on eco-literacy, sustainability and leadership. Co-organised by the Tubman.Network team.

    About the project: Youth Eco Conference

  3. 23 Feb–13 Mar

    ExchangeWhere: Mbarara, Uganda

    Okushoma kwa Boona: youth exchange in Uganda

    Six participants from Germany spent 20 days in Mbarara and, with their Ugandan partners, visited St. Helen’s Primary School (an inclusive school for blind children), Nyamitanga College, Bishop Stuart University and St. Peter’s Senior Secondary School. A delegation from BMZ and Engagement Global also visited.

    • 7 MarchFishbowl panel discussion with political decision-makers
    • 8 MarchInternational Women’s Day celebration at the Ruhaama Community Resource Centre

    About the project: Okushoma kwa Boona: youth exchange in Uganda

  4. 2024

    Conference

    Black Engagement Matters closing conference

    At the end of the UN International Decade for People of African Descent (2015–2024), the conference connected activists from Black organisations across Germany and looked at what makes Black civic engagement succeed. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: Black Engagement Matters closing conference

2023

  1. 16 December

    CultureWhere: Berlin

    FashionEmpowerment Show for Sudan Peace Action Day

    The finale of a year of workshops: children and young people of African descent showed their designs to 350 guests. African artists performed, among them artists who had come to Berlin from Ukraine. Berlin Mondiale supported the show. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: FashionEmpowerment Show for Sudan Peace Action Day

  2. April 2023–March 2024every Sunday

    CommunityWhere: Berlin

    Mama Circle: meetings for mothers who fled war

    African and Afro-diasporic mothers and female caregivers who had fled war met to share experiences and cultural traditions, with input on the health of women and children and childcare during every meeting. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: Mama Circle: meetings for mothers who fled war

  3. 2023–2024

    TrainingWhere: across Germany

    Black Engagement Matters: trainings for Black associations

    Trainings based on a needs assessment, including organisational management, legal questions, fundraising, public relations, data protection and safer and braver spaces. The plan included training Black resource persons; all activities were led by BIPoC experts. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: Black Engagement Matters: trainings for Black associations

  4. during the yearweekly

    WorkshopWhere: Salone Market, Berlin-Wedding

    FashionEmpowerment: workshops for children and young people

    Children and young people of African descent explored fashion as an expression of identity, made their own accessories with empowerment messages together with a tailor, talked about experiences of racism and learned about Afrocentric hairstyles with Ulibia Beauty Empire. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: FashionEmpowerment: workshops for children and young people

  5. during the yearweekly

    CommunityWhere: Berlin

    Equal Rights for All: cultural events and empowerment workshops

    For BIPoC refugees from Ukraine: cultural events and empowerment workshops, plus online language courses, help with job applications and psychological support. The programme was run entirely by Black people and People of Colour. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: Equal Rights for All: cultural events and empowerment workshops

  6. during the yearweekly

    CommunityWhere: Berlin

    ParentCircle: meetings for BIPoC parents

    Meetings for BIPoC parents, especially Black parents, with cooking, eating and playtime with the children. Together, the parents developed strategies for everyday life. Legal and administrative project sponsorship: Bantu e.V. · Implementation: tubman.network.

    About the project: ParentCircle: meetings for BIPoC parents

  7. 2023

    WorkshopWhere: Online

    Okushoma kwa Boona: six online seminars to begin

    At the start of the youth exchange, the twelve participants from Germany and Uganda worked on intercultural competence, Sustainable Development Goal 4 and inclusion.

    About the project: Okushoma kwa Boona: six online seminars to begin

2022

  1. September–December

    CommunityWhere: Berlin

    Ubuntu for All: welcome parties and social cafés

    For BIPoC refugees from Ukraine: five welcome parties with 300 to 400 guests each and, for three and a half months, daily social cafés with up to 200 visitors a day, plus counselling, childcare and German lessons.

    About the project: Ubuntu for All: welcome parties and social cafés

  2. May 2022–February 2023

    ArtWhere: Berlin and Kassel

    BECOMING: workshops and an installation at documenta fifteen

    A cooperation project with the foundationClass*collective of weißensee kunsthochschule berlin as part of documenta fifteen: it supported people with refugee and migration experience on their way into art school and made teaching staff aware of inclusive methods. Around 50 people took part in the workshops.

    • 15 JuneWorkshop “Tool Kit”: portfolio presentation, weißensee kunsthochschule berlin
    • 30 JuneWorkshop “Towards Inclusive Learning-Teaching Methods” for university teachers, Kassel
    • documenta fifteenBanner installation of more than 60 printed fabric banners in the rotunda of the Fridericianum in Kassel
    • 6 and 8 DecWorkshop “The eye I live in”: making artist books and zines
    • 16 DecemberWorkshop “Artistic expression in Space” with clay, glass, wood and plaster
