People

Bantu e.V. is its people: board, members, freelancers, volunteers and partner organisations in Uganda, Burundi and Berlin.

Partner organisations

We work with communities, not for them: without hierarchy and with trust.

In Uganda and Burundi

Anchored Bridges Initiatives (ABI)

Kampala, Uganda · partner since 2021

Ugandan NGO for education, inclusion, vocational training and community development, founded in 2017 as “Ability Beyond the Iris”. Joint projects: Royal College Namugongo, Ruhaama Community Resource Centre, OURS, Okushoma kwa Boona and Pearl Kindergarten.

abi.ug

Ruharo Mission Hospital (OURS)

Mbarara, Uganda

Hospital of the Diocese of Ankole. Since 1995 its OURS programme (Organised Useful Rehabilitation Services) has supported children with disabilities in western and south-western Uganda. With OURS we strengthened early detection of cerebral palsy (P 23/91).

rmh.org.ug/ours-program

Nyentsya Community Development Association (NCDA)

Mbarara, Uganda

Runs Msingi School of BTVET. With NCDA, young women completed training in hairdressing and cosmetology (P 24/200).

WIA

Women Initiative Against Vulnerability for Harm Reduction (WIV-HR)

Bujumbura, Burundi

Volunteer-run organisation in the social and health sector. Together we are bringing drinking water to Ruziba (P 26/186).

Royal College Namugongo

Namugongo, Uganda

School where, together with ABI, we covered school fees for children from poor families and built ramps for step-free access.

royalschools.ac.ug

Regenerate Africa

Kampala, Uganda

Partner for women’s coffee and cocoa cooperatives, climate and environmental protection, reforestation and ecosystem restoration.

regenerateafrica.org

Zinule: Balambuli Kwigate Coffee

Bugisu, Uganda

Partner in our coffee project: coffee from smallholder farmers in the Bugisu region on Mount Elgon who receive fair prices for their beans.

zinule.com

In Germany

AfricAvenir International e.V.

Berlin

Our partner in general education work: we design and run workshops, trainings and process support together, as well as the projects P 26/214 and P 26/224.

africavenir.org

Federation of Ugandans in Germany e.V. (FUG)

Deutschland

Umbrella organisation of Ugandan communities in Germany; cooperation partner for Ubuntu Talks 2026 in Berlin-Mitte.

fug-ev.org

tubman.network e.V.

Berlin

Organisation for children, young people, families and communities of African and Afro-diasporic heritage. An initiative under the umbrella of Bantu e.V. until January 2024; its team implemented Bantu e.V. projects from 2022 to 2024. Now an association of its own.

tubman.network

Kikili e.V. i.G.

Sandau (Elbe)

Reviving the Sandau town granary as an inclusive third place. The project is managed fully and independently by Ms Vicky Germain as developer (Bauherr*in) and project lead; Bantu e.V. has been its fiscal sponsor since 2022.

kikili.de

Each One Teach One (EOTO) e.V.

Berlin

Community-based education and empowerment organisation for People of African Descent in Berlin.

eoto-archiv.de

Wilhelm-Remy-Gymnasium Bendorf

Bendorf

Partner school: the proceeds of Aktion Tagwerk 2017 went to Uganda, among other things for ramps at Royal College Namugongo. Uganda is a topic in English lessons.

wrg-online.de
