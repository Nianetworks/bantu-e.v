Okushoma kwa Boona: Education for all!?

Twelve young people from Germany and Uganda spent almost two years (August 2023 to June 2025) exploring what prevents education for all, and what makes it possible.

OURS: early detection of cerebral palsy

172 children with cerebral palsy received rehabilitation, more than planned. Parents set up self-help groups.
