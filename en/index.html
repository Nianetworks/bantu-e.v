Non-profit association · Berlin · since 2019

Together, We Build Sustainable and Empowered Communities.

Bantu e.V. connects people in Berlin and the Great Lakes region of East Africa. With partner organisations in Uganda and Burundi we create access to education, water and participation, and in Germany we work for global justice and decolonisation.

Where we work

Two areas of work, one network

Berlin · Saxony-Anhalt · across Germany · 3 programmes · 10 projects

Work in Germany

In Berlin and across Germany we combine global learning with empowerment: education projects in schools, spaces of encounter for the African diaspora and their neighbours, advice for refugees and migrants, and fiscal sponsorship for young initiatives.

Learn more

Uganda · Burundi · 3 programmes · 12 projects

International Cooperation

With partner organisations in Uganda and Burundi we create access to education, health care, drinking water, vocational training and income. Our local partners plan and implement; Bantu e.V. secures funding in Germany, accompanies implementation and accounts for every euro.
